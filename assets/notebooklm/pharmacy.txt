Tablets
● Tablets are solid unit dosage forms containing one or
more active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs).
● They are generally prepared by compression or molding.
Tablets must possess adequate:
1. Mechanical strength
2. Physical integrity
3. Drug release characteristics
● Quality evaluation ensures consistent performance,
stability and patient acceptability.
Why evaluate tablets?
Tablet evaluation ensures:
1. Mechanical integrity during handling and
transportation
2. Resistance to abrasion and breakage
3. Appropriate disintegration after administration
4. Consistency between manufacturing batches
5. Reproducible product quality
In Process Quality Control Parameter
IPQC refers to tests and checks performed during the manufacturing process to ensure that
tablets consistently meet predetermined quality requirements.
Purpose: Detect process variations early → prevent defective batches → maintain consistent
tablet quality
Tablet Hardness
•
It is the force required to cause a tablet to fracture or break under specified conditions
• It is an indicator of tablet mechanical strength.
1. Modern instruments generally report breaking force in Newtons (N).
2. Older instruments may report values in kg/kp.
• Excessive hardness is not necessarily desirable.
• It may influence porosity and the subsequent disintegration behaviour of the tablet.
Importance of Hardness
Adequate hardness is required to:
1. Prevent tablet breakage during handling
2. Reduce damage during transportation
3. Maintain tablet integrity during packaging
4. Minimize chipping and cracking
5. Provide consistent manufacturing performance

Working:
• The tablet is placed between the jaws.

Working:

• The breaking force is read from the calibrated
scale.
Advantages: Simple, inexpensive and easy to
operate
Limitation: Manual operation can introduce

Types of hardness Testers- Digital Hardness tester
Working:
• The tablet is placed between two jaws
• Automatically increasing force is applied
until the tablet fractures
• A load cell detects the breaking force and
displays the hardness digitally
Advantages: Accurate, rapid and minimizes
operator variability with direct digital readings.
Limitations: More expensive and requires
electrical power/calibration compared with
manual testers.
Procedure for Hardness Testing
1.Ensure that the tester is clean, calibrated, and the pointer/scale reads zero before starting
2.Loosen the locking screw
3. Place a tablet vertically between the two jaws, ensuring the tablet is properly aligned
along its diameter.
4. Adjust the screw knob gently so that the movable jaw just touches the tablet surface
without applying pressure
Procedure for Hardness Testing
5.Note the initial reading if the scale does not auto-zero
6. Slowly and steadily rotate the screw knob clockwise, applying gradually increasing
compressive force on the tablet
7. Continue applying pressure until the tablet fractures/breaks into two halves
8. Observe and immediately note the reading indicated by the pointer on the calibrated
scale at the moment of fracture.
Procedure for Hardness Testing
9. Release the screw, remove the broken tablet pieces, and reset the pointer to zero.
10. Repeat the above steps for a total of 6 tablets from the same batch/sample.
11. Record all readings in kg/cm²
(convert to Newtons if required, using 1 kg/cm² ≈ 9.807 N)
Acceptance Criteria
As per pharmacopoeial and industrial practice;
Conventional compressed tablets should typically possess a hardness in the range of 4–8
kg/cm² (approximately 40–78 N),
Although the acceptable range may vary depending on tablet size, shape, and intended use
(e.g., chewable, effervescent, or sustained-release tablets may have different specifications).
What is Friability?
• Friability is the tendency of a tablet to lose particles or mass as a result of mechanical
abrasion and shock.
• It is particularly important for compressed, uncoated tablets.
• It reflects the ability of a tablet to withstand handling during manufacturing,
packaging and transportation.
• Excessive friability may result in chipping, capping, powder formation and loss of tablet
weight.
• Friability is expressed as the percentage loss in tablet weight after mechanical tumbling.
Importance of Friability.
• Friability testing helps assess tablet resistance to:
1. Abrasion
2. Repeated
3. mechanical impact
4. Handling Transportation
5. Packaging operations
Principle of Friability.
• A weighed sample of tablets is placed in a rotating drum.
• During rotation:
1. Tablets are lifted by the curved projection
2. Tablets roll/slide and fall
3. Tablets undergo repeated mechanical impact
4. Tablets collide with the drum wall and with each other
5. This produces abrasion and mechanical stress
Principle of Friability.
After rotation:
1. Tablets are
removed and
dedusted
2. Tablets are
reweighed
3. Friability is
calculated from
the percentage
loss in weight

Friability testing:
Selection criteria:
If the unit weight of tablets is less than or equal to 650mg then tablets corresponding to
approximately 6.5g total weight
If the tablet weighs greater than 650 mg in that case 10 tablets should be taken
Test conditions :
25+-1rpm x 100 revolutions (=4 min)
Typical apparatus dimensions: 287 ± 4 mm internal diameter and 38 ± 2 mm depth
The drum rotates about a horizontal axis.
Acceptance for Friability testing
For uncoated compressed tablets:
maximum mean weight loss of not more than 1.0% is generally considered acceptable.
• The presence of obviously cracked, cleaved, or broken tablets after tumbling indicates
failure of the test.
• If the result is doubtful or the weight loss exceeds the targeted value, the test should be
repeated twice and the mean of the three tests should be determined.
Introduction to Disintegration testing
•
Disintegration is the process by which a tablet breaks down into smaller particles after
coming into contact with a suitable liquid medium.
•
Disintegration is different from dissolution
•
Disintegration: tablet breaks into smaller particles.
•
Dissolution: drug molecules enter solution.
Importance of disintegration
•
Disintegration testing helps determine whether a tablet:
•
Breaks down within the specified time
•
Allows the drug-containing particles to become available
•
Has appropriate formulation performance
•
Is manufactured consistently
Importance of disintegration
Liquid contacts tablet

↓

↓

↓

↓

Factors affecting disintegrations- Test conditions

2.

3.
Apparatus conditions: Agitation/stroke frequency and movement of the apparatus
affect the mechanical stress experienced by the tablet.

Super disintegration
Super-disintegrants are highly effective disintegrating agents used at low concentrations
to rapidly break tablets into smaller particles upon contact with water
Key characteristics:
1. Rapid water uptake
2. High swelling capacity
3. Effective at low concentration
4. Promotes rapid tablet breakup
5. Enhances drug dissolution by increasing surface area
